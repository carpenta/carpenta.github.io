# Fairway Note — golf score notes

## Contract

Static GitHub Pages application for keeping one 18-hole golf round on a phone. Each hole counts swings per club (drives, approaches, putts) with one tap each, plus OB and hazard penalties and mulligans. Totals and a round analysis update on every tap.

- Hole strokes = drives + approaches + putts + OBs + hazards. Each OB and each hazard (penalty area drop) adds one penalty stroke; a replayed shot is counted as another drive or approach. Mulligans are shown but never counted.
- The hole being played counts toward totals and analysis only once it has a putt, so a tee shot alone never reads as an eagle. A hole finished without a putt (chip-in) counts once the player moves to another hole.
- A round is played on a chosen course: a bundled course with a front and a back nine, or a course typed in by hand. Pars start from the course's own published scorecard (all par 4 for a typed course) and can be set to 3, 4 or 5 per hole from the scorecard. The default is Everis Golf Resort, Saebyul nine (holes 1–9) then Lake nine (holes 10–18). Rounds saved before courses could be chosen have no `course` field; they are shown as Everis Saebyul·Lake and keep their saved pars.
- The course picker opens on the first visit with nothing saved and on "새 라운드 시작" (after its confirmation). The last choice is preselected, so starting is one tap; cancelling a new round keeps the current one. The choice is remembered in `fairway-note:prefs:v1` (course id and nine ids, or a typed name); losing it never affects the round.
- The round is saved to one localStorage key (`fairway-note:round:v1`) so it survives reloads, and is replaced when a new round starts. Opening the page writes nothing; choosing a course or the first edit saves the round and asks once for persistent storage (`navigator.storage.persist()`, failures ignored). A stored value this version cannot read (broken, or written by a newer version) is never overwritten or removed: it is first copied to `fairway-note:corrupt:<timestamp>` (newest 3 kept), and if that copy fails the round is not saved. Nothing is sent over the network; there is no account, analytics, sessionStorage, IndexedDB or cookie. Cache Storage holds only the app's own built files for offline use (see Offline). A CSP meta tag sets `connect-src 'none'` and limits scripts, styles and images to the same origin with no inline style elements (bar widths are set through the CSSOM, which CSP allows).
- Each hole stores `hazard` next to the other counts. Rounds saved before it existed have no field and read as 0, with nothing copied aside; a value that is present but not a count from 0 to 15 makes the round unreadable, as for any counter. The round stays `version: 1`. An older build of the app ignores the field: it shows such a hole without its hazard strokes and drops them when it saves, as it does with shot tags.
- Results leave the device only when the player asks: "이메일로 보내기" is a `mailto:` link with the subject and a plain-text report (totals, finished holes, analysis) and no recipient, which hands the text to the phone's mail app; "공유하기" calls the Web Share API, falling back to copying the report to the clipboard. The app itself makes no request.
- If storage is unavailable, the app keeps working in memory and says so on the entry screen.
- Saved rounds carry `lastEdited` (ms). A round whose last edit is at least 12 hours old (or, for rounds saved without `lastEdited`, one from an earlier date) is offered to continue or replace on load; replacing asks for confirmation, and continuing saves the round so the offer does not return. A round that crosses midnight is not offered. A save from another tab replaces the open tab's round through the `storage` event, so tabs do not silently overwrite each other. If another tab removes the saved round, the open tab keeps its round in memory and says it will be saved again on the next edit.
- After the first visit the app opens and records without a connection (see Offline).
- Optional shot details (see Shot details). A + tap records the stroke on its own; details never block or slow entry.

Out of scope: round history, playing partners, GPS distances, stroke indexes, other languages, putt distances.

## Course data

- `src/data/courses.json` is committed and bundled; nothing is fetched at runtime. Each course has `id`, `name` (Korean; `en` is null), `area` (picker chip), `region` (province and city), `source` (the official page the pars come from), `checked` (date) and `nines` (`id`, `name`, nine pars of 3, 4 or 5).
- A nine may also carry `holes`: nine entries in hole order, each null or an object with any of `distances` (`[{ tee, length, unit: 'm' | 'yd' }]`, the tee most players use first), `layout` (a short note such as "우 도그렉") and `guideUrl` (the hole's page in the course's own hole guide). These come from official course sites only and are being collected; none are bundled yet. `holeInfo()` finds them for a round's hole by the nine names the round keeps and leaves out a link that is not https, an empty note and a length that is not positive. A test checks any bundled notes: nine per nine, https links that are not on a denied host, positive lengths.
- `scripts/build-courses.mjs <research-dir>` builds the course list from local research notes that are not committed. A nine is bundled only when it is marked high confidence and at least one source is the course's own website (same host) or its operator's site. Values found only on commercial or crowd-sourced services whose terms forbid reuse (Hole19, GolfPass, 18Birdies, GolfingRecord, Golfzon, SmartScore, Kakao) are excluded, and so are values from GolfZon County's own site, whose terms (article 16) reserve copyright and forbid redistribution without consent; public data lists (data.go.kr) are used only to confirm the course list and hole counts, as they carry no hole pars; nothing derived from OpenStreetMap (coordinates, English names matched from it) is copied, so the bundle carries no ODbL obligations. Nines with a par outside 3–5 are skipped and reported. The script checks unique ids, nine pars per nine and that Everis has Saebyul, Lake and Pine.
- A round stores its course by display name (`{ id, name, front, back }`), so a saved round reads the same if the bundle changes. A course or nine that is no longer bundled falls back to the default course or that course's first nines when the remembered choice is offered again.
- No hole images are copied from course sites or apps. The hole is drawn by the app (see UI) and the official guide is linked instead.

## Architecture

TypeScript + Vite, no runtime dependencies, no UI framework.

- `src/round.ts` — pure model: the `Round`, `Hole` and `RoundCourse` types, the default course and course label, counter bounds (0–15), strokes, played (a hole with at least one stroke or penalty), green in regulation, totals and `analyze()`.
- `src/courses.ts` — the bundled course list, area chips, search by name, Korean initial consonants (초성) or region, the pars a choice starts with, and the optional hole notes for a round's hole.
- `src/hole-art.ts` — the drawn hole: a shape by par (and a dogleg bend read from the layout note) as numbers, and the decorative SVG made from them.
- `src/picker.ts` — the course picker, a modal `<dialog>`.
- `src/report.ts` — the plain-text report (named after the round's course), its subject and the `mailto:` link, plus the analysis notes shown on the page.
- `src/icons.ts` — static SVG icons for the three clubs (driver head, iron, putter).
- `src/storage.ts` — validates and restores a saved round and the remembered course choice. Any malformed round (wrong version, hole count, par, count or course) is ignored and a new round starts, after the value is copied aside. Malformed shot details alone do not reject the round: they are dropped, the counts are kept, and the original value is copied aside before the first save. Storage exceptions are caught.
- `src/offline.ts` — registers the service worker and shows the update prompt.
- `src/layout.ts` — publishes the sticky totals' height for the scroll offset.
- `scripts/service-worker.ts` — builds `dist/sw.js`; a small plugin in `vite.config.ts` calls it after the build.
- `src/main.ts` — the two views, the hole panel, the counters and stakes, the sheets, the scorecard and analysis, rendered from the current round after every change with DOM nodes rather than HTML strings (the icons and the drawn hole are static markup).

Analysis covers played holes only:
- putts per hole, one-putts, three-putts or worse
- greens in regulation: at least one putt after drives + approaches + OBs + hazards ≤ par − 2
- score distribution, average per par, best and worst hole (omitted when every hole has the same score against par)
- strokes lost per area against a regulation pattern of one tee shot, par − 3 approaches and two putts, plus penalty strokes (OB and hazard). Each hole adds only its own excess, so a better-than-regulation hole never offsets a loss elsewhere. Every area tied for the most lost strokes is named.

## Shot details

- Drives, approaches and putts can carry one tag per shot. Tags per counter: drive `fairway left right ob-left ob-right whiff mishit`, approach `green short long left right bunker hazard whiff`, putt `in short long left right`. Visible labels are Korean (`TAG_LABELS` in `src/round.ts`).
- Storage: optional `tags` on a hole, `{ driver?, approach?, putts? }`, each an array aligned with that counter's shots (entry i is shot i), never longer than the count and without trailing nulls; a hole with no tags has no field. `obByTag` counts the OB penalties added by OB tags. The round stays `version: 1`: older rounds read unchanged. An older build of the app reads newer rounds but drops the tags when it saves.
- An OB tag adds one OB penalty only if the OB counter does not already cover every OB-tagged drive, so an OB counted by hand is never counted twice; switching left/right OB changes nothing. Clearing an OB tag removes a penalty only if a tag added one (`obByTag`), so tag-then-clear is an exact undo. Minus on a counter drops its last shot's tag and any penalty that tag added. Minus on OB keeps `obByTag` no larger than the OB count. The approach tag `hazard` is a note only; the hazard stake is what adds the penalty.
- UI: tapping a club's count opens a bottom sheet (a modal `<dialog>`, so focus stays inside and Esc closes it) listing that club's shots on this hole, each with its chips: 48px toggle buttons with `aria-pressed` in a labelled group ("1번째"). Tapping the chosen chip again clears it. Closing returns focus to the count. A dot on the count marks a club with tags on this hole; a club with no shots has nothing to open.
- Analysis and report: `analyze()` adds tag counts over finished holes (`details`, null without tags). The scorecard view and the text report show a "샷 세부" section only when tags exist: counts per counter, drive direction left/right with OB, a note when putts were mostly short or mostly long, and the number of whiffs. Report hole lines append that hole's tags.

## Offline

- `dist/sw.js` is generated at build time with the list of every built file (`index.html`, `assets/*`, icons, manifest; not itself, source maps or dot files) and a build ID, the first 12 hex digits of a SHA-256 over the file names and contents. All paths are relative to the worker, so it works at `/tools/fairway-note/` and its scope is that folder.
- The page registers it with `new URL('./sw.js', document.baseURI)` in production builds only, when the browser supports service workers. A failed registration is ignored and the app keeps working online.
- Install fetches every listed file with `cache: 'reload'`, bypassing the HTTP cache (GitHub Pages serves `max-age=600`), into the cache `fairway-note-<build ID>`.
- Fetch is cache-first for same-origin GETs of precached files and for navigations to the tool's folder or its `index.html` (any query string), which get the cached `index.html`. A miss falls back to the network. Every other request is left to the browser.
- The worker never calls `skipWaiting` on its own. When a new worker is waiting and the page already has a controller, a small fixed button "새 버전이 있습니다 · 다시 열기" appears above the bottom bars without blocking the page. Tapping it posts `SKIP_WAITING`; the page reloads on `controllerchange`. The round lives in localStorage, so reloading keeps it.
- Activate deletes only older `fairway-note-` caches; caches of other tools on the same origin are left alone.
- The CSP is unchanged: `default-src 'self'` already allows a same-origin worker, and the worker's own fetches are not page connections.

## UI

- Mobile first, light theme for outdoor reading, system fonts, `word-break: keep-all` for Korean. Colours are grass tones: Turf `#2F7D3A` (brand, primary button, selected states), Fairway `#5BAA4F`, Rough `#3E6B2E` and Putting green `#8CCB6E` (the drawn hole), Turf ink `#17301A` (text), scorecard paper `#F3F7EF` (page), under-par red `#C0392B` (marks under par, the hazard stake) and sand `#D9B45A` (focus ring, bogey bars). The app icon, manifest and `theme-color` keep their earlier green for now.
- Two views. **기록** shows only the hole being played; **스코어카드** shows the whole round. Under 768px wide they switch with a bottom tab bar (56px plus the home-indicator inset; icon and label; the current tab in Turf); wider screens show the same two items as a switch at the top. It is a `tablist` with roving tabindex: arrow keys, Home and End move between the tabs and show their panel. The view is kept in memory, so a reload opens 기록.
- 기록, top to bottom:
  - The hole panel: ‹ ›, our own drawn hole (tee at the bottom, green at the top; a par 3 carries to a large near green, a par 4 runs up a fairway, a par 5 up a longer one to a smaller green; a dogleg named in the layout note bends it toward its side), the hole number, par as text, the first tee's length and the layout note when the course data has them, a "공식 홀 안내" link (new tab, no referrer) when it has a guide URL, and one small running total. The hole's score is shown large with golf's scorecard mark: a double circle for an eagle or better, a circle for a birdie, nothing for par, a square for a bogey, a double square for a double or worse, in red under par. A hole in progress shows its count without a mark.
  - The card: three counters side by side, 드라이버, 어프로치 and 퍼터, each with its icon and label, the count (tap for details), a 56px + and a 48px −. Under them two stakes: OB (white with a dark band) and 해저드 (red), each adding one penalty stroke with a count badge and a − beside it once it has a count (hidden but holding its place, so nothing moves). Mulligans are behind a quiet "더보기" sheet.
  - "다음 홀" right above the tab bar; on the last hole it reads "스코어카드 보기". ‹ › and 다음 홀 only move between holes; nothing is recorded by moving. Moving shows the top of the screen.
  - The whole screen (header, hole panel, card, 다음 홀, tab bar) fits 375×553 (iPhone SE Safari) without scrolling, and no view scrolls sideways at 360px. Tap targets are at least 48px with 8px between neighbours. A disabled button keeps its label with a lighter fill rather than fading.
- 스코어카드: sticky totals (strokes and score against par, holes, putts, penalty strokes, mulligans; one line on screens 700px tall or less), front and back nine tables with the scorecard marks, the par source link, the analysis, sending and "새 라운드 시작". Tapping a hole number opens it in 기록; tapping its par opens a small sheet with Par 3, 4 and 5. The scroll offset follows the totals' measured height (`--summary-h`).
- A problem with saving (storage refused, the saved round removed in another tab, an unreadable value copied aside, shot details left out) is shown on 기록 until the next edit, and on the scorecard's save line.
- One polite live region announces each change ("3번 홀 퍼터 2, 5타", "1번 홀 해저드 1, 2타") and each hole change; text is rewritten only when its value changes.
- Small labels are at least 13px and 4.5:1 against their background for sunlight. While the page is visible it holds a Screen Wake Lock (feature-detected, errors ignored, re-requested on `visibilitychange`); it needs no network, so the CSP is unchanged. The back link to the site (`../../`) is small and low in the header on every screen. On screens 440px tall or less (landscape) the totals are not sticky.
- `touch-action: manipulation`, no tap highlight and no text selection on buttons, so rapid taps never zoom or select. Safe-area insets pad the header, page and bottom bars.
- A web app manifest and PNG icons (rendered once by `scripts/make-icons.mjs`) let the page open standalone from the home screen.
- The course picker fills the screen: search, area chips, a scrolling course list, then the chosen course, its nines and a 52px start button, all within 375×553 with no horizontal scroll. Start is focused on open so the keyboard does not appear. The chosen course is shown under the app title and in the report.
