# Fairway Note — golf score notes

## Contract

Static GitHub Pages application for recording 18-hole golf rounds on a phone, one at a time, and keeping the finished ones on the device. Each hole counts swings per club (drives, approaches, putts) with one tap each, plus OB and hazard penalties and mulligans. Totals and a round analysis update on every tap.

- Hole strokes = drives + approaches + putts + OBs + hazards. Each OB and each hazard (penalty area drop) adds one penalty stroke; a replayed shot is counted as another drive or approach. Mulligans and bunkers (v14, 상세) are shown but never counted.
- The hole being played counts toward totals and analysis only once it has a putt, so a tee shot alone never reads as an eagle. A hole finished without a putt (chip-in) counts once the player moves to another hole, or finishes the round with 라운드 마치기 (v14): opening the summary sets `closed: true` on the round, so the hole being played counts too, also in past rounds and after a reload; recording on that hole again or moving to another clears it, a par change does not.
- A round is played on a chosen course: a bundled course with a front and a back nine, or a course typed in by hand. Since v45 a course typed in by hand can be kept as 내 코스, with its pars, nine names and an optional handicap index, in its own key (see 내 코스). Pars start from the course's own published scorecard (all par 4 for a typed course) and can be set to 3, 4 or 5 per hole from the scorecard. The default is Everis Golf Resort, Saebyul nine (holes 1–9) then Lake nine (holes 10–18). Rounds saved before courses could be chosen have no `course` field; they are shown as Everis Saebyul·Lake and keep their saved pars.
- The course picker comes first on open: with nothing saved, with a saved round that has nothing recorded (older versions saved an untouched round on open), and with a round from an earlier day. For an earlier-day round its back button reads "지난 라운드 이어서" and continues that round; starting a new course from it asks before the earlier round is replaced (a round with a finished hole is kept among past rounds first), and closing it leaves the earlier-day banner. It also opens on "새 라운드 시작" (after its confirmation). The last choice is preselected, so starting is one tap; cancelling a new round keeps the current one. The choice is remembered in `fairway-note:prefs:v1` (course id and nine ids, or a typed name); losing it never affects the round.
- The round is saved to one localStorage key (`fairway-note:round:v1`) so it survives reloads, and is replaced when a new round starts, after a round with a finished hole has been moved to the past rounds (see Past rounds and backup). Opening the page writes nothing; choosing a course or the first edit saves the round and asks once for persistent storage (`navigator.storage.persist()`, failures ignored). A stored value this version cannot read (broken, or written by a newer version) is never overwritten or removed: it is first copied to `fairway-note:corrupt:<timestamp>` (newest 3 kept), and if that copy fails the round is not saved. Nothing is sent over the network; there is no account, analytics, sessionStorage, IndexedDB or cookie. Cache Storage holds only the app's own built files for offline use (see Offline). A CSP meta tag sets `connect-src 'none'` and limits scripts, styles and images to the same origin with no inline style elements (bar widths are set through the CSSOM, which CSP allows).
- Each hole stores `hazard` next to the other counts. Rounds saved before it existed have no field and read as 0, with nothing copied aside; a value that is present but not a count from 0 to 15 makes the round unreadable, as for any counter. The round stays `version: 1`. An older build of the app ignores the field: it shows such a hole without its hazard strokes and drops them when it saves, as it does with shot tags.
- Results leave the device only when the player asks: "이메일로 보내기" is a `mailto:` link with the subject and a plain-text report (totals, finished holes, analysis) and no recipient, which hands the text to the phone's mail app; "공유하기" calls the Web Share API, falling back to copying the report to the clipboard. The round summary's 공유 sends the round as a PNG drawn on the device where the browser can share files, and the report as text otherwise; where the image cannot be shared it can be saved as a download (see Share card). The app itself makes no request.
- If storage is unavailable, the app keeps working in memory and says so on the entry screen.
- Saved rounds carry `lastEdited` (ms). A round whose last edit is at least 12 hours old (or, for rounds saved without `lastEdited`, one from an earlier date) is offered to continue or replace on load; replacing asks for confirmation, and continuing saves the round so the offer does not return. A round that crosses midnight is not offered. A save from another tab replaces the open tab's round through the `storage` event, so tabs do not silently overwrite each other. If another tab removes the saved round, the open tab keeps its round in memory and says it will be saved again on the next edit.
- After the first visit the app opens and records without a connection (see Offline).
- Optional shot details (see Shot details). A + tap records the stroke on its own; details never block or slow entry.
- Optional one-line entry, 말로 입력 (v21), off until turned on in the menu (see Voice entry). The keyboard's own dictation turns speech into text in a plain text field; the page reads only that text, parses it on the device and drops it. It never asks for the microphone or uses the browser's speech recognition (Web Speech), and the CSP is unchanged. Where speech is processed is the keyboard's choice, and the sheet says so: on an iPhone dictation runs on the device; an Android keyboard may send it to a server. Only the switch is stored (`voice: true` in the prefs key).
- Finished rounds are kept in a second key, `fairway-note:history:v1`, with trends and a read-only view of each. They move between devices only in a backup file that the player saves or shares; the app never uploads it, and importing one never replaces the round being recorded without the player's yes (see Past rounds and backup). Since v43 the prefs key also keeps when the last backup was made (`backedUpAt`, a local date) and how many rounds were kept then (`backedUpRounds`): the menu's 백업과 복원 line says it, and after a round is kept the backup card asks only with no backup yet, five rounds kept since the last, or the last thirty days old (`src/backup-nudge.ts`). Opened from the home screen, the backup panel reads `navigator.storage.persisted()` and says in one line whether the browser keeps the site's storage.

Out of scope: playing partners, GPS distances, other languages, putt distances, automatic sync between devices.

## Course data

- `src/data/courses.json` is committed and bundled; nothing is fetched at runtime. Each course has `id`, `name` (Korean; `en` is null), `area` (picker chip), `region` (province and city), `source` (the official page the pars come from), `checked` (date) and `nines` (`id`, `name`, nine pars of 3, 4 or 5, and optional `holes`).
- `scripts/build-courses.mjs <research-dir>` builds it from local research notes that are not committed. A nine is bundled only when it is marked high confidence and at least one source is the course's own website (same host) or its operator's site. Values found only on commercial or crowd-sourced services whose terms forbid reuse (Hole19, GolfPass, 18Birdies, GolfingRecord, Golfzon, SmartScore, Kakao) are excluded, and so are values from GolfZon County's own site, whose terms (article 16) reserve copyright and forbid redistribution without consent; public data lists (data.go.kr) are used only to confirm the course list and hole counts, as they carry no hole pars; nothing derived from OpenStreetMap (coordinates, English names matched from it) is copied into this file. The Everis hole outlines (v27) are OpenStreetMap-derived and live apart, in `src/data/osm/` under ODbL (see Hole shapes from OpenStreetMap). Nines with a par outside 3–5 are skipped and reported. The script checks unique ids, nine pars per nine and that Everis has Saebyul, Lake and Pine.
- Hole notes: a nine may carry `holes`, nine entries in hole order, each `null` or a `HoleInfo` (`distances`, `layout`, `guideUrl`, `si`; see `src/courses.ts`). The script merges them from local per-nine hole research, and from the hole fields of the official Gyeongsang research (whose nines cite only the course's own pages), under the same rule as the pars: the page the notes were read from must be on the course's own or its operator's site, and a link is kept only as an https page on that site.
  - Lengths need a stated unit (m or yd): one per named tee (metres when both are published), or the single length a course publishes with no tee name (no `tee`). The tee most players start from (white, else regular, blue or front) comes first, with both its greens on a two-green hole (A/B or left/right, each tee's greens kept together); other tees follow a fixed order, and tees a course names only by number come highest first.
  - Dropped: lengths outside the par's range (par 3 60–280 m, par 4 180–560 m, par 5 300–650 m), holes whose tees all show one length (placeholders), every length of a course whose pages do not state the unit clearly (Cypress gives the same numbers in m and yd; Ansung's card prints none), and every length of a Gyeongsang nine whose research notes say its own pages disagree about lengths (totals that do not add up, tees out of order, m and yd that do not convert).
  - Stroke indexes are kept as published when the nine has 1–9 once each, or 1–18 once each together with another nine of the course; otherwise the nine gets none.
  - A layout note keeps at most three short phrases within 40 characters, a dogleg phrase first so the drawn hole bends; the phrases are short descriptions written from the guide, never copied guide text.
  - Everything left out is counted by reason in the script's output.
- At runtime `holeInfo()` finds a round's hole notes by the nine names the round keeps, keeps a tee-less length and a handicap index from 1 to 18, and leaves out a link that is not https, an empty note and a length that is not positive.
- A round stores its course by display name (`{ id, name, front, back }`), so a saved round reads the same if the bundle changes. A round has 18 holes, or 9 for one nine played on its own (v11): then `back` is empty, and a 9-hole round with a back nine named or with no course is unreadable (a truncated 18-hole round stays broken). The choice may carry `holes: 9` in the prefs key. A course or nine that is no longer bundled falls back to the default course or that course's first nines when the remembered choice is offered again.
- No hole images are copied from course sites or apps. The hole is drawn by the app (see UI), from OpenStreetMap's outlines where it has them (v27), and the official guide is linked instead.

## Hole shapes from OpenStreetMap (v27)

Everis Golf Resort's holes are drawn from OpenStreetMap (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0). OSM maps its greens, tees, fairways, bunkers and water in detail but has no `golf=hole` lines, so the holes were matched by hand.

- Data: `src/data/osm/everis.json`, a Derivative Database under ODbL kept apart from `courses.json` (nothing in either takes from the other; OSM's own `par` values are never used). For each matched hole: its line of play from the back tee to the middle of its greens, simplified outlines of its two greens, its tees, fairways, bunkers and the water near it, in metres east and north of the greens' middle (one decimal), and the OSM element ids used. `src/data/osm/README.md` states the licence, the attribution, the Overpass query and the data date (OSM base 2026-11-20T14:28:25Z).
- Matching: `src/data/osm/everis-holes.json` maps each hole to its OSM tees, greens and fairways with the evidence. The three nines start and end 95–160 m from the clubhouse, their holes follow one another on the ground (each green 52–102 m from the next tees), the pars in that order match the official cards, and each hole's notes agree where OSM shows the feature (the island green, the island tees, ponds on the named side). The two shapes OSM tags `golf=tee` at the end of 새별 5 are drawn as its greens (512–540 m² like the course's greens, ringed by bunkers). 레이크 4 (no green in OSM; that spot is mapped as construction) and 레이크 5 (374 m to the nearer of two green pairs against the official 456 m) are left on the schematic.
- Tolerance: every matched hole's par equals the official one and its line of play is within 8% of the official length (−6.9% to +4.4%; the widest gap is 새별 8, which has no tee further back in OSM).
- Building: `scripts/osm-holes.mjs`, run by hand (`bun run osm:holes <extract>`), never in the build or CI, reads one saved Overpass answer and the mapping. One projection for the course (the ellipsoid's radii at the course's latitude); Douglas–Peucker per kind (tees 0.6 m, greens 0.4 m, bunkers 0.5 m, fairways 0.8 m, water 1 m); the line of play takes the middle of the widest fairway across the tee→green axis every 8 m, smoothed and simplified (a par 3 is straight); a hole's bunkers are those in its rough or within 12 m of its greens or fairways, its water any within 25 m of its play. It prints each hole's measured length beside the official one.
- Loading: the file is its own chunk of the build (77.9 kB, 29 kB gzipped), from the app's folder and precached like the rest, loaded the first time a round on Everis is drawn (`src/osm-holes.ts`); other courses never fetch it, and the drawing waits for it rather than show the schematic first. If the chunk cannot load, the course keeps the schematic.
- Drawing (`osmShape`, `osmSvg` in `src/course-art.ts`, v33): the hole is turned once (cached per hole) so its tee→green axis runs up, then a quarter turn lays it across the band 기록 shows (see The band), the back tee at the left and the player's left up. One scale fits the tees, the greens in their fringes (1.1 times), the fairways with their 4 px cut, the bunkers and the flag into the largest room the band leaves beside the credit's touch area, and centres them there; water is the setting, and the band's edges cut it. The flag goes on the green nearest the line of play. The 100 m and 150 m markers stand at their distance from the greens' middle along the line of play, where the dot and its pill fit the band clear of the flag, the greens, the tees, the credit and each other. (v27–v31 ran the axis up the screen's middle, the back tee behind 다음 홀 and the pin where the schematic's stood, so on 기록 the card hid the tee and a bunker behind 새별 1's greens sat under the Par chip.) The outlines are rounded by corner cutting (twice for greens and tees, once for the rest), drawn in the schematic's flat classes (v31); a fairway's and a green's outline is written once in `<defs>` and its first cut and fringe are `<use>` copies of it.
- Credit: whenever an OSM hole is drawn, "© OpenStreetMap" links to https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright (new tab, no referrer) as quiet gray text 6 px above the card (v33; a dark pill at the card's top-left until v31): 13 px at the help weight in `--on-dark-quiet` (white and ink, 5.3:1 on the rough), no fill, edge, rounding or shadow, its left edge on the card's content edge where the first capsule starts. The band fit keeps the hole out of its touch area, so the rough is behind it. No gray reads on water (`--water` #3d86a3: white is 4.1:1), so where water would lie behind its text the hole moves within its room's slack (2 px steps, nearest first) until none does, and failing that the credit stands at the band's other end, its right edge on the card's right content edge (`CREDIT_SIDES`; `.end`). Over Everis's 25 holes at the four measured bands it stands in the left alone kept water from behind it in 97 cases of 100, the right alone in 94, either as needed in 99; 새별 7 in 상세 at 390×844 keeps a sliver at either end (0.1% of the line box, a corner), listed and pinned by the unit test so it cannot grow, and 홀 안내 says the drawing is OpenStreetMap's with the same link; 앱 정보 names the data and its licence. A link is navigation, so the CSP is unchanged. OSM is drawn only where the band under the header row (and any banner) is fitted, 50 px or more (v33; 30 px for the pill in v27–v31); a shorter band (360×640, 375×667's 상세, 375×553, where the header's small picture leads) keeps the schematic and shows no credit.
- Cost (measured in headless Chromium at 390×844, 3× scale): turning a hole 0.07 ms once; its shape and SVG 0.14 ms; the markup 14–26 kB (median 19 kB, about 120 nodes) against the schematic's 9 kB and 75; a hole change's synchronous work 1.6 ms median (2.0 ms p95) against 1.3 ms on a schematic course, with no frame over 50 ms in 102 changes.

## Architecture

TypeScript + Vite, no UI framework. The one runtime dependency is a bundled font (see Font).

- `src/round.ts` — pure model: the `Round`, `Hole` and `RoundCourse` types, the default course and course label, counter bounds (0–15), strokes, played (a hole with at least one stroke or penalty), green in regulation, totals and `analyze()`.
- `src/courses.ts` — the bundled course list, area chips, search by name, Korean initial consonants (초성) or region, the pars a choice starts with, and the optional hole notes for a round's hole.
- `src/course-art.ts` — the hole drawn behind the screen: a shape by par and hole (and a dogleg bend read from the layout note) as numbers, and the decorative SVG made from them. Since v22 it also reads the bunkers and water a layout note names (`layoutHazards`, phrase by phrase; `holeHazards` for a par) and places them by rule (see UI). Since v27 it also draws a hole from OpenStreetMap's outlines (`orientedHole`, `osmShape`, `osmSvg`), sharing the schematic's gradients and patterns.
- `src/osm-geo.ts` — plane geometry for those outlines (v27), with no imports so the build script shares it: the projection, Douglas–Peucker for lines and rings, ring area, centre and containment, line length and the point a distance from its end, the line of play through the fairways, and the turn that puts a hole's tee at the bottom.
- `src/osm-holes.ts` — the OpenStreetMap outlines by course (v27): loads a course's file once on first use (its own chunk) and finds a round's hole by the nine names the round keeps.
- `scripts/osm-holes.mjs` — builds `src/data/osm/everis.json` from a saved Overpass answer and the hand-made mapping (v27); run by hand.
- `src/version.ts` — the app version, shown under 앱 정보 in the menu only (v29: no longer on 기록 or the picker); each improvement round adds one (`docs/IMPROVEMENTS.md`).
- `src/picker.ts` — the course picker, a modal `<dialog>`.
- `src/report.ts` — the plain-text report (named after the round's course), its subject and the `mailto:` link, plus the analysis notes shown on the page.
- `src/share-card.ts` — the round as a PNG to share (see Share card): its numbers, a pure layout of drawing operations and a small canvas painter.
- `src/share-round.ts` — the summary's 공유 with the share card (v16): draws it as the summary opens, the preview, file sharing with the text and copy fallbacks, and 이미지 저장. `main.ts` only calls it when the summary opens, closes and is tapped.
- `src/icons.ts` — one icon family (v14): a 24px grid, a 1.75px line (2.6 below 16px) with round caps and joins, in currentColor; club heads carry an 18% tint. The penalty glyphs (v31) are golf objects in the same family, coloured by class: OB a white post on a grass mound, 해저드 the same post in red at water, each post a lit face and, inside its line, a shaded side (white alone was 1.1:1 on its button, an empty outline), 벙커 a bean of sand seen from above (up to v30 they read as a pencil, a crayon and a pair of lips; grains on the sand read as a cookie). They grow with the text: 24px, 28px in 큰 글씨; 20px and 24px in a short screen's 상세 tiles. Our golf glyphs are drawn here (the clubs, the bunker, the putter in the finish check); since v25 the UI icons the scripts draw (the capsules' + and −, the 상세 pad's arrows, the club names' chevron, the round buttons' share, copy, download and mail and the preview's badge) are Tabler files, each imported by name (see Icons).
- `src/tabler.ts` — `tablerInner()` (v25): a Tabler file's shapes without its wrapper or its invisible bounding box. `icons.ts` uses it, and so does a small plugin in `vite.config.ts` that fills `index.html`'s empty `<svg data-icon>` placeholders with the named files' shapes and writes Tabler's MIT notice beside the built files.
- `src/shot-panel.ts` — the club detail panel (see Shot details): the chip layout per club, `chooseTag()`, and the dialog. Since v28 a panel is a view of a tag list (`VIEWS`: 드라이버, 그린까지, 세컨, 어프로치, 퍼터): `viewRange()` gives its shots in the list (세컨 and 어프로치 share the middle count's), and `chooseViewTag()` writes a chip there. An approach has no 벙커 chip (v18): 상세's 벙커 counts bunkers, so one is never recorded twice; `LEGACY_TAGS` keeps the live site's approach 벙커 readable, its chip shown only on a shot that carries it, to see or clear. It wraps each club's count in a button and borrows the hole, the club names and a way to change the hole from `main.ts` through one `ShotPanelHost`; `main.ts` calls `bindShotPanel()` once and `renderShotPanel()` after every render.
- `src/voice-commands.ts` — 말로 입력's parser (v21), pure: `parseLine(text, holes)` reads one line of Korean as commands for the hole being played (set or add a count, one hole move at the end, or undo), or says why it changes nothing; `failureText()` words that. No DOM and no storage.
- `src/voice-view.ts` — 말로 입력's menu switch, its button on 기록 and its sheet. It borrows the hole count, the club names and `applyLine` from `main.ts` through one `VoicePage`, so a line goes through the taps' own functions.
- `src/storage.ts` — validates and restores a saved round and the remembered course choice. Any malformed round (wrong version, hole count, par, count or course) is ignored and a new round starts, after the value is copied aside. Malformed shot details alone do not reject the round: they are dropped, the counts are kept, and the original value is copied aside before the first save. Storage exceptions are caught.
- `src/offline.ts` — registers the service worker and shows the update prompt.
- `scripts/service-worker.ts` — builds `dist/sw.js`; a small plugin in `vite.config.ts` calls it after the build.
- `src/fonts.css` and `src/fonts/` — the bundled Pretendard (v24, see Font): three `@font-face` rules, imported once from `main.ts` before `style.css`, and the unmodified woff2 files with their licence, which another small plugin in `vite.config.ts` writes beside the built fonts.
- `vendor/tabler/` — Tabler Icons 3.52.0 outline SVGs with their MIT licence (see Icons): the build draws the 23 that `index.html` names, the scripts import the 12 they draw, and `check`, `golf` and `upload` are unused.
- `src/history.ts` — past rounds: the history key, reading every entry with the same checks as the round key, copying an unreadable history aside, keeping a round once, merging, ordering and trends.
- `src/backup.ts` — the backup file: building it, reading and checking it, and planning an import.
- `src/history-view.ts` — `renderHistory(container, page)` mounts the past rounds view (trends, list, status) into any container and builds the dialog for one round on first use; `keepBeforeNewRound()` and `newRoundQuestion()` serve every way into a new round. It renders when the history changes, never on a tap.
- `src/backup-view.ts` — `renderBackup(container, page)` mounts the backup controls (save, share, import, status) into any container.
- `src/dom.ts` — the small element builder and text helpers shared by the page and both views.
- `src/main.ts` — the two views, the hole panel, the counters and stakes, the sheets, the scorecard and analysis, rendered from the current round after every change with DOM nodes rather than HTML strings (the icons and the drawn hole are static markup). It lends its scorecard (read-only columns, no hole buttons), analysis and share code to both views through one `Page` object, so a past round reads like the current one, and mounts them into `#history` and `#backup`.

Analysis covers played holes only:
- putts per hole, one-putts, three-putts or worse
- greens in regulation: at least one putt after drives + approaches + OBs + hazards ≤ par − 2
- score distribution, average per par, best and worst hole (omitted when every hole has the same score against par)
- strokes lost per area against a regulation pattern of one tee shot, par − 3 approaches and two putts, plus penalty strokes (OB and hazard). Each hole adds only its own excess, so a better-than-regulation hole never offsets a loss elsewhere. Every area tied for the most lost strokes is named.

## Shot details

- Drives, approaches and putts can carry one tag per shot. Tags per counter: drive `fairway left right far-left far-right long short ob-left ob-right whiff mishit`, approach `green short long left right bunker hazard whiff`, putt `in short long left right`. Visible labels are Korean (`TAG_LABELS` in `src/round.ts`).
- Storage: optional `tags` on a hole, `{ driver?, approach?, putts? }`, each an array aligned with that counter's shots (entry i is shot i), never longer than the count and without trailing nulls; a hole with no tags has no field. `obByTag` counts the OB penalties added by OB tags. The round stays `version: 1`: older rounds read unchanged, and a round saved by the live site before the redesign (no hazard field, tags on approaches and putts) reads whole, with nothing copied aside, and keeps its tags when saved again (unit-tested). An older build of the app reads newer rounds but drops the tags when it saves.
- An OB tag adds one OB penalty only if the OB counter does not already cover every OB-tagged drive, so an OB counted by hand is never counted twice; switching left/right OB changes nothing. Clearing an OB tag removes a penalty only if a tag added one (`obByTag`), so tag-then-clear is an exact undo. Minus on a counter drops its last shot's tag and any penalty that tag added. Minus on OB keeps `obByTag` no larger than the OB count. The approach tag `hazard` is a note only; the hazard stake is what adds the penalty.
- UI, the club detail panel (클럽 상세, v17, `src/shot-panel.ts`): a tap on the club's name above its capsule (v20: a 44px button with the club's icon, its name and a chevron, and a dot once the club has details on this hole; the user chose it once the capsule became all + and −) opens a modal `<dialog>` with that club's chips for the shots on this hole. The capsule's halves stay one tap each and never open it; a long press on the capsule still opens the 0 … 5+ picker, whose release lands on the picker, so no click reaches the halves.
  - Chips sit on one six-column grid, three across or two across, with an 8px gap both ways: row one is left, the target (페어웨이, 온그린 or 들어감; 온그린 for the tee shot on a par 3) and right, row two 김 and 짧음 (as on the 상세 tee line), then the rest with left things on the left and right things on the right (drive: 많이 좌, 헛스윙, 많이 우 and 좌 OB, 뒤땅/토핑, 우 OB; approach: 벙커, 해저드, 헛스윙). Direction chips carry the 상세 pad's arrows. Every tag of `SHOT_TAGS` appears once (unit-tested).
  - The panel opens on the last shot; from two shots on, a row of shot numbers (five across at most) picks which shot the chips write, with a dot on shots that have a tag. One tap sets the chosen shot's tag, the same chip again clears it, and with no shot counted a chip counts the first one, as the 상세 pad does (`chooseTag`). Only choices are filled: a chosen chip in fairway with a turf ring, the chosen shot in turf, a chosen OB chip in sand; OB chips are outlined in sand because they may add a penalty.
  - It closes with ×, a tap on the backdrop (which presses nothing under it) or Escape, and focus returns to the count. The count is a button named "드라이버 2, 샷 세부" ("… 샷 세부 1개" with tags), and a 7px turf dot at its top right marks a club with tags on this hole (under the digit it read as punctuation).
  - Geometry: left and width from the card's inner edges (the sheet's content box, measured on its unmoving wrapper), so it spans the steppers; top under the club's pill, or higher so it ends 8px above the tab bar. Radii are concentric (24px outside, 12px padding, 12px chips); targets are 44px to the eye and 48px with their touch areas. It rises 6px over 160ms like the other small popovers, with no motion under reduced motion.
  - In 상세 the tee-shot pad and 미스샷 row still write the first drive's tag; the panel shows the same value.
- Analysis and report: `analyze()` adds tag counts over finished holes (`details`, null without tags). The scorecard view and the text report show a "샷 세부" section only when tags exist: counts per counter, drive direction left/right with OB, a note when putts were mostly short or mostly long, and the number of whiffs. Report hole lines append that hole's tags.

## 세컨 and 어프로치 (v28)

The middle count is every shot after the tee shot until the ball is on the green. 기본 shows it as one capsule, **그린까지** (the user's name for it; 아이언 in v20–v26, 어프로치 before). 상세 shows it split in two: **세컨**, the full shots toward the green (layups included), and **어프로치**, the short shots around it (chips, pitches, bunker shots).

- Storage: the stored key stays `approach`, the whole middle count, so strokes, totals and every combined number read as before. A hole may also carry `short`, the 어프로치 part (0 … `approach`); 세컨 is `approach − short`. `short` is present once the hole's split is known and missing otherwise: on holes entered in 기본 and in every round saved before v28. `short: 0` is meaningful (all of it was 세컨). The round stays `version: 1`. A round from the live site (v18) or v26, and a backup file of them, read unchanged with nothing copied aside (unit-tested on hand-written JSON of both). A `short` that is not a count within `approach` is dropped like a bad tag: the hole and its score are kept and the original value is kept aside. The history fingerprint appends `:s<short>` only on split holes, so every fingerprint made before v28 is unchanged and a backup made earlier still matches its kept round. An older build reads v28 rounds and drops `short` when it saves (as with tags).
- Changes: in 상세, 세컨 ± changes `approach` only; 어프로치 ± changes `approach` and `short` together. The first change to 세컨 or 어프로치 on a hole whose split is not known records it (`short: 0`, all of it 세컨, then the change); a tap on 드라이버, 퍼터, a penalty or the tee pad does not. The long press sets a part straight to 0 … 5+ and records the split even when the count is already right ("어프로치 0" says it was all 세컨). In 기본, 그린까지 ± on a split hole moves 세컨, and once 세컨 is 0 its − takes an 어프로치, so `approach` never drops below `short`. The middle count stops at 15 with both parts together.
- Shot details: the middle count's tags stay one list, 세컨's shots first. A 세컨 more goes in at the end of the 세컨 part (later tags move along with their shots), a 세컨 less takes the last 세컨 and its tag, an 어프로치 is added and taken off at the end. The 세컨 panel shows shots 1 … 세컨 of the list, the 어프로치 panel the rest (a hole entered in 기본 is all 세컨), and 기본's 그린까지 panel all of them. 세컨 offers the middle count's chips as before; 어프로치 offers its own: 좌 · 붙음 · 우, 김 · 짧음, 칩인 · 뒤땅/토핑 (`close`, `in` and `mishit` were added to the list's `SHOT_TAGS`). A tag a panel does not offer (the live site's 벙커, or another panel's) shows only on a shot that carries it, to be seen and cleared. Each part's panel says what it is under its name. In the analysis, tags on split holes count under 세컨 or 어프로치 by the shot that carries them, and under 그린까지 on the others.
- Analysis: every combined number stays as it was (strokes, greens in regulation, strokes lost against one tee shot, par − 3 and two putts). On finished split holes only, `analyze().split` adds: how many holes are split; 세컨 and 어프로치 shots; strokes lost by 세컨 against par − 3 and by 어프로치 against none; greens in regulation by the split (a putt, no 어프로치, and drives + 세컨 + penalties ≤ par − 2); and up and downs (chances: holes with an 어프로치; made: one 어프로치 then one putt, or none for a chip-in). 분석 shows 세컨 and 어프로치 as indented rows under 그린까지 in 타수를 잃은 곳 with one line under the chart naming the holes they count, a "세컨·어프로치" row of three tiles (나눈 홀, 그린 적중, 업앤다운) with a line defining both, and two sentences in 정리. The summary and the share image add 업앤다운 as their last tile or stat, only once an 어프로치 is recorded.
- Text and files: report hole lines write the middle count as 그 (어 before v28) and a split hole as `그2(세1 어1)`, with that hole's tags by part (`· 세 온그린 · 어 붙음`); the header names 그린까지(세컨 + 어프로치) when any hole is split, and [분석] adds the split holes' greens and up and downs. The CSV appends 세컨 and 어프로치 as its last two columns, so the columns a sheet made earlier reads stay where they were; they are blank on holes not split, and their 합계 counts the split holes. The analysis text has both columns after 그린까지 ("-" where not split) and the split holes' lost strokes, greens and up and downs.
- Voice: 그린까지 sets the whole count, and so does 아이언 in a line without a part. 세컨 (세컨드, 세컨샷) and 어프로치 (어프로치샷, 칩샷) set their part as the long press does. In a line that names a part, 아이언 is only the club it was hit with ("세컨 아이언으로 하나"), and "7번 아이언" is never a count. A line with both 그린까지 and a part changes nothing and says to say them one at a time. The sheet's example names the parts in 상세.
- UI: see 기록 under UI.

## Past rounds and backup

- Storage: `fairway-note:history:v1` holds a JSON array of `{ id, endedAt, round }` in the order rounds were kept. `id` is a random UUID (time plus random digits where `crypto.randomUUID` is unavailable) and survives export and import; `endedAt` is ms since the epoch; `round` is read with the same checks as the round key (hazard counts and shot details included). An entry needs at least one finished hole. The key appears with the first kept round and is removed with the last.
- Unreadable values: a stored history that is not a JSON array, or has an entry that fails the checks, repeats an id, carries unknown fields or loses shot details when read, is copied to `fairway-note:corrupt:<timestamp>` (the round key's slots: newest 3 kept, the same value never twice) on load and before every write. If the copy fails, the history is not written and the section says so.
- Keeping a round: every way into a new round ends in `startRound` once a course is chosen, and that is where a round with a finished hole is added to the history, just before it is replaced; cancelling the picker or going back from it ("지난 라운드 이어서") keeps nothing. "새 라운드 시작" and the earlier-day banner ask "지금 라운드를 지난 라운드에 보관하고 새로 시작할까요?"; the picker that opens first for an earlier-day round asks the same about "<date> 라운드". A round with no finished hole is not kept, and the question says so. A round already kept (same date and the same par, counts, tags and OB link on every hole, whatever the key order or the hole being shown) is never added again, so a crash between keeping and clearing cannot duplicate it. If the history cannot be written, a second question asks whether to start anyway; only that yes loses the round.
- List and trends: newest first by round date, then by when kept. Each row shows the date, the course as the page labels it, strokes and score against par, finished holes, putts and OB. Trends use full 18-hole rounds only, so a short round never lowers the average: rounds kept (and how many are full), average strokes and against par, the latest 5 once there are more than 5, putts and OB per round, greens in regulation as a share of holes, and a line of the latest 10 rounds' strokes. The line is an inline SVG built with DOM APIs, with its values in an `aria-label`; since v32 it carries a dot per round (a round cap on a path of no length, so it stays round on the stretched line), the first and last rounds' days under its ends (`trends()` returns each point's day), and a vertical scale of at least ten strokes, so 82 then 83 is no steep climb. The trends' tiles follow the analysis's rule for an odd count (below).
- One round: a modal `<dialog>` the size of the viewport (at most 640px wide) with a sticky title bar and close button, built by the view on first use and appended to `<body>`. It shows the page's scorecard read-only (v23: the same nine-column tables as 스코어카드, white on the dialog's paper, each hole's column a plain element with a screen-reader line, 16px between the nines) with the total line and the analysis, then the summary's round buttons (v23: 52px, 8px apart, from the body's content edge): 공유 or 결과 복사 with the round's image (see Share card), 이미지 저장 where the image cannot be shared as a file, and the round's `mailto:` report; it deletes after a confirm. The close button and Escape close it; the page behind does not scroll while it is open.
- Backup file: `fairway-note-backup-YYYY-MM-DD.json` (local date) holding `{ app: "fairway-note", format: 1, exportedAt, current, history }`. `exportedAt` is ISO 8601; `current` is the round being recorded, or null when nothing is entered; `history` is the stored entries. It is saved through a Blob URL and `a[download]`. Where `navigator.canShare({ files })` accepts a JSON file, a second button hands the same file to the Web Share API. Both work offline and make no request; the CSP is unchanged.
- Import: `<input type="file" accept="application/json,.json">`; files over 5 MB are refused before reading. A file that is not JSON, not `app: "fairway-note"`, or not `format: 1` with a `history` array changes nothing and says so; a higher format asks for a reload to get the newer app. Every entry and the `current` round get the same checks as stored ones, and failures are counted. Entries join the history unless their id or the same round is already there. The backup's round in progress, if new here, replaces the round being recorded only after a confirm, which first keeps the current round when it has a finished hole; declining files it among past rounds instead. The status line reports imported, skipped and unreadable counts.
- Entered rounds (v44): "넣기" in the 지난 라운드 header opens the course picker, then a sheet that takes the day (today at most) and strokes per hole. Every cell starts at par; a tap picks a cell and a chip sets it and moves to the next, so a bogey round takes about thirty taps. The chips run from −2 to +7 in two rows of five (v47; −1 to +3 before), so an eagle, a hole-in-one on a par 3 and a double par (양파) can be entered. A round on the same day and course already kept is flagged before saving, not refused. `enteredRound()` stores it in the round format unchanged (`version: 1`): each hole's strokes in `approach`, every other count 0, `closed: true`, `current` on the last hole, and `entered: true`, which `readRound` keeps only when it is exactly `true`. `analyze()` reports `entered`; the views show strokes and the total only, and the trends average strokes and par over every full round but putts, OB and greens over recorded rounds only (`Average.recorded`; tiles hidden when there are none). The score line draws entered rounds as open dots. The kept entry's `endedAt` is 18:00 on the day played. After saving, the v43 backup rule decides whether the backup card shows.
- Every round as one CSV (v44): the backup panel saves `fairway-note-rounds-YYYY-MM-DD.csv` (UTF-8 with BOM, CRLF) with a row per finished hole of every kept round: `날짜,코스,전반,후반,홀,파,타수,퍼트,OB,해저드,넣은 기록`. Entered rounds leave putts, OB and hazards blank and read `예` in the last column. Cells are quoted like the per-round CSV. It is export only; import stays the JSON backup.
- Right after a round is kept from the summary, 지난 라운드 shows a backup reminder card (v10): "라운드를 보관했어요", one sentence on why, "백업 파일 저장" (the backup view's own download) and "나중에"; after saving it reads "백업 파일을 저장했어요" with 닫기. It is held in memory only and hides the home-screen card while it shows.
- Once a round is kept, 지난 라운드 shows a one-line home-screen card (v9) between the "지금 라운드" card and the list: "방법 보기" expands why (browsers may clear sites not opened for a while) and the iPhone and Android steps; "알겠어요" puts it away for good (`home: true` in the prefs key). It is not shown when opened from the home screen (`display-mode: standalone`), and nothing pops up on 기록.
- Where they are: the list mounts in the 지난 라운드 tab under a "지금 라운드" card (the round being recorded: course, date, holes recorded, score, 이어서 기록), and the backup controls in the menu's 백업과 복원 panel. Each view only needs a container, so it moved there unchanged. Rows and buttons are at least 44px tall, and the list, the dialog and the menu fit 360px without horizontal scrolling and 375×553 with the dialog filling the screen.

## 내 코스 (v45)

- Why: the bundle covers a small share of the country's courses, and a course typed in by hand kept nothing, so every round there started at par 4 again (and so did an entered past round, v44).
- Offer: when the summary opens for a round on a course typed in by hand, `saveOffer()` returns 'save' when no kept course has its name, 'update' when one does with other pars for the same number of holes, and null otherwise (a bundled course, the default name "직접 입력", nothing recorded). The card sits last in the summary's body, on its column, 8px from the buttons when scrolled to the end; once saved it says so until the summary next opens.
- Saving: a sheet asks the nines' names once (전반·후반 by default, one name for a nine-hole round, 20 characters each) and, behind a "HCP(선택)" disclosure styled like the app's others, a handicap index per hole in the 넣기 grid. All empty or all filled; filled, it must hold 1–9 once in each nine or 1–18 once over eighteen holes (`validHcp()`), else one line says so and nothing is saved. 'update' rewrites only the pars of the kept course of that name.
- Storage: `fairway-note:courses:v1` holds `[{ id, name, front, back, par, hcp? }]`, at most 50: `id` is `my-` plus a random UUID and survives backup and import; `par` is 18 values (or 9 for a nine-hole course, whose `back` is empty) of 3, 4 or 5; `hcp` has the same length and passes the same check. A stored list this version cannot fully read is copied to `fairway-note:corrupt:<timestamp>` before it is written. The prefs key keeps the chosen kept course as `mine` beside `course: null` and its name; an older version ignores it and starts a typed-in course of that name.
- Rounds are unchanged: a round on a kept course is `{ id: null, name, front, back }` with the kept nine names, so it reads anywhere a typed-in round does. `findMine()` finds its kept course by name and nine names (one nine, or a nine-hole course played twice), and that course gives 파 수정 its reference pars (`publishedPar()`) and the hole its "HDCP n" (`holeInfo()`); with the course deleted, the round shows as before.
- Picker: kept courses come first under a "내 코스" heading (read aloud too), then "골프장"; the search finds them by name, an area chip hides them. Choosing one starts with its pars and nine names (`roundSetup()`), on 18 holes or 9 as toggled. A name typed last time that is now a kept course opens chosen, its heading scrolled into view. 넣기 (v44) goes through the same picker, so its cells start at the kept pars.
- Deleting: with a kept course chosen, the panel under the list shows its nines with 지우기 in the place 전·후반 바꾸기 takes for a bundled course, then "내 코스, Par n" (and "HCP 있음"). It asks first, leaves past rounds as they are, and leaves the name as a course typed in by hand. The picker's 내 코스 group is the list; the menu gets no row, so it still fits a 390×844 screen whole (v36).
- Backup: the file adds `courses` when there are any, still `format: 1` (an older import ignores the field). Import reads them with the stored list's checks and adds them unless the same id, or the same name and pars, is already kept; the same name with other pars is added as "name (2)".

## Share card

`src/share-card.ts` draws a round as a 1080×1350 (4:5) PNG to share. Since v16 the round summary's 공유 sends it (see How it is wired), and since v23 a past round's 공유 too.

- What it shows: the course and its nines (or "<nine> 9홀"), the date, the total with its score against par in a scorecard mark (a red disc under par, plain 이븐 at par, a sand square over par), 전반/후반 with the holes and par counted, each nine as columns of hole, par and score with the app's marks (double circle eagle or better, circle birdie, none for par, square bogey, double square double, filled square triple or worse; a 9-hole card adds putts), sand nine subtotals, then putts, greens in regulation, fairways, penalties and the best hole, and a small "Fairway Note" wordmark with the app icon. Deep turf with mowing stripes behind a paper scorecard.
- The numbers are the summary's: `analyze()` over finished holes, a 9-hole round is one whole nine with nothing on the other, fairways are par 4 and 5 tee shots with a 상세 result. Unfinished holes show "–" and no mark.
- `shareCardContent(round)` gives the numbers, `shareCardLayout(round, measure?)` the drawing operations (pure; the browser measures text with the canvas, tests with a fixed estimate) and `drawShareCard(round): Promise<Blob>` paints them on a canvas and encodes a PNG with `toBlob`. No images, so it works offline and needs no CSP change.
- Type (v25): the page's Pretendard. Canvas text never waits for a font (it measures and draws in whatever has loaded), so `drawShareCard` first awaits `document.fonts.load()` for the two faces it uses, 600 for labels and 800 for values (`SHARE_WEIGHTS`; the layout's type allows no other), for at most 1.5 s, after which the system font stands in as on the page. Until v24 it drew in the system stack, with 700s that Pretendard would match to its 800 face; those are now 600 (the nines, a subtotal of putts, an unfinished hole's dash, the wordmark) or 800 (a nine's score against par). The tests' width stand-in (`estimateWidth`) is wider than Pretendard for words and up to 3% narrower for a short number at 800; the browser measures the real text.

How it is wired (v16, `src/share-round.ts`; since v23 `createRoundShare({ save, status, container, row, preview?, image?, shareText })` makes one instance for the summary and one for the past-round dialog, with the same behaviour; `dressRoundButtons` gives 공유 the share glyph where there is a share sheet and the copy glyph where it can only copy, with a name to match, and draws 이미지 저장 and mail from the icon family):

- Drawing: when the summary opens, after its next paint (`requestAnimationFrame` then a task), so opening never waits for the canvas. The card is kept as a `File` named by `shareCardFileName(round)` (`image/png`) and as a `data:` URL, and let go when the summary closes. A round with no finished hole has no image.
- Preview: 56×70 (4:5) at the inner right edge of the summary's hero row, centred on the score ring's line and smaller than it, with `--lift` for depth. Until the card is drawn it is a plain deep-turf block (`data-state="drawing"`, the image's own ground, no shimmer); the image then fades in over 0.2 s (none with reduced motion). It is shown as a `data:` URL because `img-src 'self' data:` blocks `blob:` images. A badge in its corner shows what a tap does: share, or save where files cannot be shared. Because the buttons below do the same, the preview is `aria-hidden` and out of the tab order.
- 공유: where `navigator.canShare({ files: [file] })` accepts the PNG, `navigator.share({ files: [file], title, text })` with the report's subject and text. Once the card is drawn (as it is when the preview shows) the call happens within the tap, which iPhone Safari needs: it refuses a share that has lost the user's activation. Where the share sheet takes only text, 공유 shares the text report as before; with no share sheet it copies it (결과 복사). A second tap while a share is open is ignored.
- 이미지 저장: wherever the image cannot be shared as a file (text-only share sheet, or none), a round button with a down arrow sits after 공유 or 결과 복사. It saves the PNG through an object URL on an `a[download]` inside the dialog, revoked after a minute, as CSV 저장 does.
- Failures: closing the share sheet (`AbortError`) says nothing; any other failure says so in the status line. If the canvas cannot draw, the preview and 이미지 저장 are hidden and 공유 sends the text.
- The summary still fits 375×667 without scrolling: the preview sits in the hero row rather than a row of its own, and the action row is a flex row of 52px round buttons with 보관 taking the rest, so three or four buttons share the same edges and 8px gaps.

## Voice entry (말로 입력, v21)

- Why a text field: on phones, Web Speech hands the voice to the browser's or the system's recogniser, which may send it to a server, and the page can neither require on-device recognition (Chrome's `processLocally` is desktop only) nor see where it went; a home-screen web app on iOS may have no Web Speech at all. The keyboard's dictation needs nothing from the page and leaves the promise as it was: the page receives text. Bundled WASM speech models are deferred (tens of MB, a looser CSP, unclear licences).
- Grammar (`parseLine`): "<club> <number>" sets that count (decision D1: the same as the long press, and a line heard twice changes nothing more), as in 드라이버 하나 그린까지 둘 퍼터 두 번. A club, OB, 해저드, 벙커 or 멀리건 said alone adds one, as does a line saying it happened (OB 났어요); 더 adds, 빼 takes off, 없음 or 0 is none, and 다섯 이상 or 5+ reads like the long press's 5+. Club words: 드라이버, 드라이브, 티샷; 그린까지, and 아이언 (its v20–v26 name) in a line without a part; the parts 세컨, 세컨드, 세컨샷 and 어프로치, 어프로치샷, 칩샷 (v28, see 세컨 and 어프로치); 퍼터, 퍼트, 퍼팅, 펏; OB, 오비; 해저드, 워터 해저드, 페널티 구역; 벙커; 멀리건. Numbers: counting words (하나 … 스물), the forms before 번 (한, 두, 세, 네 …), digits, Sino-Korean (영, 일 … 이십) and golf talk with the number first (투 퍼트, 3퍼트; never 포, which is also the shout). One move may end the line (다음 홀 or 다음, 이전 홀, 7번 홀, 칠 번 홀, 일곱 번째 홀, 첫 홀); 되돌리기 or 취소 is said alone.
- Dictation's spelling: any spacing, digits or words, particles (드라이버는, 그린까지가, 퍼터를) and endings (두 번이요, 쳤어요), fillers and joining words (음, 그리고, 하고, 랑) and punctuation, after NFC, half-width digits and o.b. → ob. Every reading of the line is tried (a small backtracking parser, memoised by position); the line passes only when the readings that pass all mean the same.
- Traps: 이번 홀 (this hole) is never 이 번 홀 (hole 2); 오비 is OB, not 오 (5) and 비; 세컨샷 is not 세 (3); 네 is four only after a club; "9홀" is not a move (it is as often nine holes); "5번 아이언" is a club, not a count.
- Not guessing: a negation (안 났어, 아니, 못, 말고, 없었어) changes nothing, and so do unknown words (the message names the word as written), two readings (퍼터이: a particle or 2), counts above 15, holes outside the round (1–9 in a 9-hole round), a move that is not last or a second one, and 되돌리기 with anything else. The message sits under the field, the line stays selected so the next dictation replaces it, and nothing is saved.
- Applying (`applyLine` in `main.ts`): each count through the function its tap uses — + and − (`count`), a penalty button (`addPenalty`, so 빼기 still takes off the last one added), the long press (`setCount`; it reads 5 as 5+, so an exact 5 under a higher count steps to 4 and adds one), the menu's − and + for 멀리건 — then the move as its button does it (`goNext`, with 라운드 마치기 on the last hole and the blank-hole notice; ‹; a jump as from the hole strip). One undo pill says what was set in the card's club names, "드라이버 1 · 그린까지 2 · 퍼터 2" (after "3번 홀" when the line moved on), and its 되돌리기 puts the hole back and, after a move, returns to it: `restore()` now restores the hole before going back, which leaves the blank-hole notice as it was. A move alone shows no pill, like its button. The live region reads the line with the hole's strokes. Before changing anything it refuses what cannot be done: nothing to undo, 이전 홀 on hole 1, the hole already being played.
- The sheet holds the last change's 되돌리기 while it is open (the 4-second timer waits), so "되돌리기" said there still finds it; closing it with nothing applied starts the 4 seconds again. It drops the line however it closes, at once and not on a late `close` event, so a quick reopen keeps what is newly said.
- Layout: the button is a 52px white circle (`--next-h`, the menu button's look) on 다음 홀's row, its left edge on the card's and the menu button's inset; 다음 홀 keeps its right edge and gives up the button's width and an 8px gap. ## Offline

- `dist/sw.js` is generated at build time with the list of every built file (`index.html`, `assets/*` with the three font files and the font's and the icons' licences, icons, manifest; not itself, source maps or dot files) and a build ID, the first 12 hex digits of a SHA-256 over the file names and contents. All paths are relative to the worker, so it works at `/tools/fairway-note/` and its scope is that folder.
- The page registers it with `new URL('./sw.js', document.baseURI)` in production builds only, when the browser supports service workers. A failed registration is ignored and the app keeps working online.
- Install fetches every listed file with `cache: 'reload'`, bypassing the HTTP cache (GitHub Pages serves `max-age=600`), into the cache `fairway-note-<build ID>`. ## Font (v24)

- Pretendard 1.3.9 is bundled so text has the same widths, heights and baselines on every phone. - The drawn hole's distance labels (SVG text) inherit the page font, and since v25 the share card draws in it too, once its two faces have loaded (see Share card).
